Sunday Pandesal

by Joyce Manalo

Synopsis

A baker's family chronicles one ordinary, perfect Sunday.

Chapter 1: Warm From the Oven

Before sunrise, Lolo lit the oven. The scent of yeast crept through the sleeping street like a gentle alarm.

By seven, the neighbors queued with coins and gossip. Nothing special happened, and that was the whole beauty of it.
