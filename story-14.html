The Great Lechon Heist

by Marco Villar

Synopsis

Four cousins plot to steal their own family's fiesta lechon.

Chapter 1: Operation Crispy

It started as a joke at Tita Baby's dinner. By Saturday, the cousins wore black shirts and carried a ladder.

The lechon was gone before they arrived. On the table sat a note: 'Nice try. Love, Lola.'
