Whisper in the Well

by Gabriel Tan

Synopsis

A village well answers questions, but always takes a payment.

Chapter 1: What the Water Wants

The old women warned the children away, but Dani leaned over the rim and asked where her missing brother was.

The well answered in his voice, close and cold. 'Come down and see.' Her reflection smiled before she did.
