Poetry

Verses for the Typhoon Season

by Lourdes Aguilar

Synopsis

A cycle of short prose-poems about waiting out storms with family.

Chapter 1: When the Rain Speaks

The roof drums. The radio crackles with names of provinces. Mother lights a candle, and the shadows learn to behave.

We tell old stories until the wind gets bored. By morning the world is washed, and we are all still here.

— End of chapter —
