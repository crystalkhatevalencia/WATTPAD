The Clockmaker's Alibi

by Isabel Ferrer

Synopsis

Every clock in town stopped at 9:14, the minute a man vanished.

Chapter 1: Nine Fourteen

Inspector Salas wound the final pocket watch and listened. It ticked backward. Whoever stopped the town clocks had wanted him to notice the one thing they missed.

In the workshop floor, beneath a loose board, lay a ledger written in a code only a child could crack.
