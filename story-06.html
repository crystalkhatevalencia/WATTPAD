Case of the Missing Mango

by Paolo Santos

Synopsis

Detective Dingdong investigates who stole the fruit stand's prize mango.

Chapter 1: Peel Back the Clues

The Barangay fruit stand was in uproar. The golden mango, grown for the fiesta, had vanished overnight, leaving only a trail of tiny sandal prints.

Dingdong crouched, sniffed the air, and smiled. 'Someone small, someone hungry, and someone who left the evidence on their chin.'
