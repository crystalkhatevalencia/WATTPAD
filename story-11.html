Compass of Forgotten Isles

by Rico Batungbakal

Synopsis

A map-maker's son sails to find the islands his father erased.

Chapter 1: The Edge of the Chart

Tomas unrolled the final map and found a blank sea in the middle. His father's hand had scratched out an entire archipelago.

He set sail at dawn with a stolen compass that pointed not north, but toward whatever he feared to lose.
