Mother's Silence

by Elena Navarro

Synopsis

A daughter returns home to learn why her mother stopped speaking.

Chapter 1: The Kitchen Table

The kitchen smelled exactly as Rosa remembered, of garlic and old rain. Her mother stirred the pot and said nothing.

Rosa sat down and began to talk, filling the quiet with twenty years of unsent apologies. Slowly, her mother reached across the table.
