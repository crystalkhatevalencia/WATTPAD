Synopsis

Two strangers swap misdelivered letters and slowly fall in love.

Chapter 1: The Wrong Mailbox

Camille's postcard from Lisbon arrived in Manila three weeks late, addressed to someone named Rafael. She almost threw it away, until she read the first line.

He wrote back within the week, apologizing for his terrible handwriting. She kept that reply under her pillow.
