Bakunawa's Appetite

by Lakan Dimaculangan

Synopsis

A fisherman bargains with the moon-eating dragon of old legends.

Chapter 1: The Seventh Moon

The village beat pots and pans whenever the Bakunawa rose, but Isko climbed the cliff alone with an offering.

'Spare the sixth moon,' he begged. The dragon tilted its enormous head. 'What will you give me in return?'
