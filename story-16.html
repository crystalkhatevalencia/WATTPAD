The Galleon's Last Song

by Elias Ramos

Synopsis

A cabin boy on a Manila galleon sees the ship's final voyage.

Chapter 1: Across the Pacific

Joaquin was twelve when he boarded the galleon, small enough to climb the masts and curious enough to count the stars.

On the ninetieth night, the stars vanished. The captain began to sing, and the sailors knew the sea had other plans.
