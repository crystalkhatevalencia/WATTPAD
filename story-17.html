The Ninth Floor

by Cassandra Lim

Synopsis

An office elevator stops at a floor that does not exist.

Chapter 1: Floor Nine

Dana pressed eight, but the elevator hummed past it and opened onto a corridor of unfamiliar doors.

Each door bore a name from her past. At the end of the hall, one door bore her own, and it was already open.
