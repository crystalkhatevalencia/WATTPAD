Rain on Rizal Avenue

by Jun Dela Cruz

Synopsis

A jeepney driver and a night-shift nurse share one umbrella and one dream.

Chapter 1: Last Trip of the Night

The rain hammered the roof as Elena climbed aboard at 2 a.m., scrubs soaked. Ramon pretended not to notice her shaking hands and turned up the heater.

'Same stop?' he asked. She nodded. For the first time in months, the long road home felt short.
