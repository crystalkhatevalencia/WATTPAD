The Bear Who Borrowed Winter

by Nina Salazar

Synopsis

A bear who hates sleeping borrows winter to stay awake.

Chapter 1: A Season on Loan

Bruno was tired of missing the snow. So he asked Old Winter if he might borrow it for one week, just to enjoy.

Winter agreed, with one condition: whatever Bruno borrowed, he had to return exactly as he found it.
