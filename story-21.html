The Princess Who Hated Glass Slippers

by Ysabel Cortez

Synopsis

Princess Ayla would rather fix carriages than dance at balls.

Chapter 1: The Royal Mechanic

Ayla's wardrobe overflowed with gowns she never wore. Her real treasure was a wrench hidden beneath her pillow.

When the royal carriage broke on the eve of the ball, only one person could repair it, and she was wearing grease.
