No Signal

by Ryan Aquino

Synopsis

A hiker's phone receives texts from a number that was disconnected years ago.

Chapter 1: One Bar

The first text arrived at the ridge: 'Don't take the east trail.' The sender was her brother, dead since 2019.

Maya ignored it. Then the east trail forked, and the message came again: 'I told you.'
