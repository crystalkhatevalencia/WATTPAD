The Hollow at Elm Street

by Veronica Cruz

Synopsis

Every house on Elm Street is lit at night, except the one that watches.

Chapter 1: Number Thirteen

Nobody remembered the thirteenth house being built. It simply stood there, dark windows reflecting nothing, even at noon.

Mia counted the houses again from her bedroom. Twelve. She turned around, and the curtains behind her breathed.
