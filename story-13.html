My Cat Runs for Mayor

by Bea Lacson

Synopsis

When the mayor resigns, a cat named Mr. Whiskers wins the vote.

Chapter 1: Paws for Politics

Nobody expected the write-in campaign to succeed. Yet there was Mr. Whiskers, napping on the podium, with 4,203 votes.

His first act was banning Mondays. His second was demanding tuna in the budget. The town has never been happier.
