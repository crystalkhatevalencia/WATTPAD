Dragonfly Spell

by Theo Alcantara

Synopsis

A hedge-witch's apprentice turns into a dragonfly and cannot turn back.

Chapter 1: Wings at Midnight

Nico meant to shrink only his homework. Instead the incantation hummed through his bones, and the garden grew enormous around him.

Master Odessa found him on a lily pad, wings trembling. 'Well,' she sighed, 'at least you finally learned to listen to the wind.'
