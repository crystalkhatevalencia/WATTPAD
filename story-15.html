Fall of the Silver Gate

by Alexander Reyes

Synopsis

A mapmaker's daughter smuggles plans out of a besieged city.

Chapter 1: Night of the Siege

Smoke curled over the walls of Alvira as Sofia sewed the map into her cloak lining, stitch by trembling stitch.

At the Silver Gate, a guard stopped her. She met his eyes and recited her father's last instructions, word for word.
