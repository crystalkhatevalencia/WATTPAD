Seven Hours to Baguio

by Daniel Ortega

Synopsis

Two estranged brothers drive to their father's funeral.

Chapter 1: The Long Road

Neither spoke as the bus terminal faded behind them. Luis gripped the wheel, Mateo stared out the window at mountains.

Somewhere near Kennon Road, Mateo laughed, remembering their father's terrible singing. Luis laughed too, and the miles forgave them.
