Daughter of Thunder

by Maria Concepcion

Synopsis

A storm god's daughter is exiled to earth with only a borrowed name.

Chapter 1: Fallen Lightning

Lightning cracked across the sky, and a girl landed in a rice field, steaming and furious.

The farmers gave her a hat, a bowl of rice, and the name Dalisay. She hated both, until the first rain came.
